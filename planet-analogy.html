The AIOS as a planet

A personal AI operating system has four layers. The point of the nesting is that each layer rests on the one beneath it — you build from the core outward. Hover a layer to highlight it.

Inner core Core context

Who the user is, what they do, their goals and priorities, the people and businesses they care about. Everything downstream depends on this.

A library of short markdown documents about the user.

Mantle Extensions — connections, tools, references

The AIOS's reach outside its own files: authenticated connections (Gmail, Slack, GitHub), general-purpose tools (a headless browser, a CLI generator), and external knowledge bases the AIOS draws on.

Gmail wired in via the Google Workspace CLI.

Outer shell Workflows

Partially or fully automated workflows built on top of the inner core and the mantle. Start by automating slices of a task, with the human in the loop, and grow the share over time.

Drafting a LinkedIn post in the user's voice; turning a video idea into a script, slides, and an edit.

Atmosphere Always-on, 24/7/365

The AIOS operating even when the user is away from their computer. The payoff layer — don't try to build it before the inner three are real.

An AI executive assistant answering coworkers in the user's voice at 2 AM; talking to the AIOS from a phone via Telegram on a walk.
